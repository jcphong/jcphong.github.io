About

I'm an AI/ML engineer who builds and ships production AI systems, from agentic workflows and LLM applications to computer vision and identity verification.

I enjoy turning research ideas into reliable products: designing the system, making models fast enough for real users, and measuring that they actually work.

What I build

  • Production agents

    Agentic workflows with tool calling, model routing and human-in-the-loop, plus the evals, tracing and guardrails that keep them reliable.

  • Fast, efficient LLM serving

    Inference that is quick and affordable at scale: batching, caching, quantization and the right serving stack.

  • Document AI & RAG

    Vision-language models that read PDFs, scans and forms, feeding hybrid retrieval and reranking for grounded answers.

  • Identity & on-device AI

    Face recognition, liveness and OCR for identity verification, with lightweight models that run on phones.
